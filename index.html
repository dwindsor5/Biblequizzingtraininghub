Welcome to the Bible Quizzing Training Hub

Practice for Bible quizzing anywhere, using the Bible text your team is studying. Every question is built from your own source text, so it always matches what you are learning.

What you can do here

Practice Questions
Random questions in every quiz type: Interrogative, Multiple Answer, the Reference types, Quote, Finish and Situation. Pick the types you want to work on, race a 30-second timer, and get confetti for right answers and olives for misses.

Jump Mark Practice
Train to jump at exactly the right word. The verse is read one word at a time, you press JUMP at the earliest safe spot, then finish the verse. Only jump marks for the chapters in your source text show up.

Where your text is saved
Share the site with everyone

Makes a copy of the whole site with this source text built in. Put that file on a free web host (like Netlify) and anyone with the link can practice, with no Claude account. When you change the text, download a new file and upload it to the same place: everyone gets the update. People can practice with the text but they cannot see or change it on the site.

It understands the usual ways Bibles are copied: book titles, “Chapter 5” or “John 5” headings, verse numbers inside the text (including tiny raised numbers), and “5:1” style references. Section titles and footnote marks are skipped. If something is off, fix it in the box and save again.
